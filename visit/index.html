Before you come

A few things
to know.

Have a question about visiting, Sunday School, or something you need for your visit? Reach out to the church.

What time is the Sunday service?

Our Sunday worship service runs from noon to 2:00 pm at 1844 Teri Road in Austin.

Can I watch online?

Yes. The church’s Facebook page carries the Sunday livestream, listed at 12:30 pm. You can also find messages on YouTube.

Is there something for children?

Kids for Christ is our Sunday School ministry. Children learn about Jesus through Scripture and creative activities. Contact the church for current Sunday School details.

Can I ask about transportation or access?

Please use the church contact form to ask about transportation, accessibility, or any arrangements you need before your visit.

How do I share a prayer request?

Use the church prayer request form to share what you would like prayer for.

How can we pray for you?

Share a prayer request with the church. We would be glad to stand with you in prayer.
