Who we are

A local church.
A living faith.

International House of Prayer Church Dove Springs is a Bible-believing, Holy Spirit-filled church in Austin, Texas.

We are committed to sharing the love of God through His Son, Jesus Christ. Prayer, worship, Scripture, fellowship, and outreach shape our life together.

We pray for awakening and revival in our community, our city, and the United States.

What we believe

Our foundation
is the Word.

  • The BibleWe believe the Bible is God’s Word and the authority for our faith and life.
  • Father, Son, and Holy SpiritWe believe in one God, revealed in three persons: the Father, the Son, and the Holy Spirit.
  • Jesus ChristWe believe in Jesus Christ, His divinity and humanity, His bodily resurrection, and His return.
  • Grace and faithWe believe salvation comes by God’s grace through faith in Jesus Christ.
  • Life in the SpiritWe believe in the Holy Spirit, spiritual gifts, the church, water baptism, and the Lord’s Supper.
